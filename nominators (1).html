Not just in your organisation. In the economy. In the world.

How it works

What the 10 months actually look like

Two 5-day in-person gatherings in Paris anchor the program — one to set the stage, one to close it. In between, the cohort meets online twice a month, three hours at a time, to go deep on one of six themes shaping the world. Three Champions of Impact conferences bring outside voices into the room, and a personal mentor plus a peer coaching group walk with your nominee throughout — not just during the ten months, but after.

What RESET stands for

Five ideas define the core of the program

01

Regeneration

Rebuilding rather than just sustaining — strategies that replenish natural, social, and economic systems.

02

Entrepreneurship

Driving active, creative agency — turning systemic insight into tangible, thriving ventures.

03

Systems

Mapping complexity — seeing the interconnected dynamics behind isolated symptoms.

04

Ethics

Refusing easy compromises — bringing philosophical and moral inquiry into executive decisions.

05

Trust

Rebuilding the relational glue — psychological safety, peer accountability, transparent alliances.

Whom to nominate

Four signs someone belongs in this cohort

01

Leaders with a voice inside them

They feel the growing gap between the system they operate in and the leader they want to be. They haven't found the right space to act on it yet — but the conviction is there. You can see it.

02

People who want to act, not just think

They're done with analysis and good intentions, and are looking for the guidance, tools, and community to make change real.

03

Builders of a different future

They look at their organisation, sector, or the world, and know it can do better. That belief is fuel, not frustration.

04

Seekers of depth and connection

They want a genuine transformation — a shift in how they think, lead, and show up — and are ready to do the work it requires.

Why RESET, and not another program

Four commitments that set this apart

Not problems taught, but problems sat with

Most programs give frameworks. We ask participants to first understand and feel a problem, and let it change how they think.

Multiple lenses, not one

Business, political, psychology, ethics, ecology, economics — real change needs more than one way of seeing.

People, not just experts

Inspiring professionals, indigenous communities, and local changemakers whose way of seeing the world challenges what participants thought they knew.

Mentored during and after

Every participant is guided throughout the program and supported beyond it — their pledge is only the beginning.
